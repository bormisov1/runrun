Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Location and run history

RUNRUN uses your device location to record routes, distance, speed and elevation during a run. The 24-hour location history feature is on by default. With background location permission, it also records your route when no run is active, including while the app is in the background or your phone is locked. You can select part of that route to create an activity. If background permission is unavailable, location is recorded only while RUNRUN is open. The operating system may stop updates when you force close the app.

Recent location points are stored only on your device and are removed as they pass 24 hours. You can turn this feature off in RUNRUN Settings at any time; doing so clears its recent points. Activities you create from those points remain in run history until you remove the app and its local data. RUNRUN does not upload routes or run history to a developer server. You can export a route yourself using the share controls.

Camera, microphone and photos

Camera and microphone access are used only when you choose to take a photo or record a video. Audio is included in videos if you grant microphone access. Captures are saved to your device's photo library when you choose Save. You can also use your device's share sheet to send an export to a destination you select.

Data sharing

RUNRUN does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics trackers. We do not sell your location, run history, photos or videos. Exports you share are handled by the destination you select and are subject to its privacy practices.

Permissions and choices

You can revoke location, camera or microphone permissions in your device settings. Without location access, route recording will not work. Without camera or microphone access, related capture features may be unavailable.
