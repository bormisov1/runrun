Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Location and run history

RUNRUN uses your device location to record a route, distance, speed and elevation during a run. If you choose background recording, location continues to be collected while an active run is in progress, including when the app is closed or your phone is locked. Recording stops when you finish or discard the run. You can choose recording only while the app is open.

Routes and run history are stored on your device. RUNRUN does not upload them to a developer server. You can discard an active run, export a route yourself, or remove the app and its local data using your device settings.

Camera, microphone and photos

Camera and microphone access are used only when you choose to take a photo or record a video. Audio is included in videos if you grant microphone access. Captures are saved to your device's photo library when you choose Save. You can also use your device's share sheet to send an export to a destination you select.

Data sharing

RUNRUN does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics trackers. We do not sell your location, run history, photos or videos. Exports you share are handled by the destination you select and are subject to its privacy practices.

Permissions and choices

You can revoke location, camera or microphone permissions in your device settings. Without location access, route recording will not work. Without camera or microphone access, related capture features may be unavailable.
